# Surface brief: personal publishing platform

- Mode: public Read/Experience; admin Operate.
- Primary user: the owner publishing articles, projects, and games.
- Primary visitor action: open a piece of work and understand it quickly.
- Signature interaction: three making lanes on the public home; ledger rows and one explicit save action in the admin.
- States: draft, published, archived, empty list, invalid login, duplicate slug, responsive mobile layout.
- Boundaries: one administrator, SQLite, Docker Desktop, no comments or multi-user roles in v1.
